Triangles fall into a six-sided bin. Grab any point where six triangles meet and turn them round it. Get all six to one colour and they close into a hex and clear.

Every six hexes, a new colour joins the bin, from two up to eight, and the triangles come faster. Let the bin fill and the game is over.

THREE MODES
• MAIN: clear 48 hexes, bring in all eight colours, and win.
• ENDLESS: no end. Just you and the bin.
• SPRINT: three minutes. Score what you can.

Points climb with every new colour in play. Look out for glowing triangles: clear one and it either drains the bottom of the bin or turns every triangle of one colour into another.

SIX SKINS TO UNLOCK
Every MAIN win, and every milestone of lifetime hexes cleared, opens the next skin. Each one brings its own palette, type and sound:
• base: dark and bright, the original
• riso: risograph ink and paper grain
• crt: green phosphor and scanlines
• flash: heavy outlines and old-school colour
• mono: greyscale with one red
• acid: one strobing colour at a time

BUILT TO READ AT A GLANCE
Every colour carries its own mark (a dot, a ring, spokes and more), so you never have to tell pieces apart by colour alone.

LEADERBOARDS
Every mode has its own Game Center leaderboard. Sign in to see where you rank. You can play without signing in.

PLAY IT YOUR WAY
• Portrait or landscape, on iPhone and iPad.
• Touch: tap a point to grab it, tap again to turn. Use the turn buttons, or twist with two fingers. In landscape, tap low on either side to turn.
• Game controllers: Xbox, PlayStation, MFi and other extended gamepads, with rumble. On PlayStation controllers, the light bar glows the newest colour.
• Keyboards: WASD or the arrow keys to move, Q and E to turn, 1, 2 and 3 to start a mode.
• Haptics, plus a synthesised sound that changes as each colour joins.

NO ADS. NO TRACKING. NO PURCHASES.
Hexgen works offline and collects no data. Game Center is optional.
