How to play

Triangles fall into a six-sided bin. Grab a point where six triangles meet and turn them around it. When all six are one colour, they close into a hex and clear. Every six hexes adds a new colour, up to eight. The game ends when the bin fills.

  • MAIN: clear 48 hexes to win.
  • ENDLESS: play until the bin fills.
  • SPRINT: three minutes.

Skins

The first skin is open from the start. Each MAIN win, and reaching 50, 150, 300, 600 and 1000 lifetime hexes, unlocks the next one. Tap SKIN on the menu to switch.

Common questions

Can I move my progress to a new device? Not yet. Scores and skins are stored only on the device.

Does it work offline? Yes. Only Game Center leaderboards need a connection.

How do I see the leaderboards? Sign in to Game Center in Settings, then tap the Game Center button on the Hexgen menu.

Can I play in landscape? Yes, on iPhone and iPad. In landscape, tap low on the left of the screen to turn back, or low on the right to turn forward.

Is there sound? Yes. Check that your device isn't on silent.
