Privacy

Hexgen for iPhone, iPad and the web · updated 16 October 2026

Hexgen collects no data. The only thing that ever leaves your device is your score, and only if you've signed in to Game Center.

What stays on your device

Your best score in each mode, how many hexes you've cleared in total, how many MAIN games you've won, and which skin you picked. The game needs these to show your best scores and unlock skins. They're kept in the app's local storage (in the browser, for the web version) and are deleted when you delete the app or clear site data.

Game Center

On iPhone and iPad, if you're signed in to Game Center, the score from each finished game is sent to Apple's Game Center leaderboard for that mode, under your Game Center nickname. Apple runs Game Center, and its use of your data is covered by Apple's Game Center privacy notice. We don't store your Game Center identity or scores anywhere else. If you don't want to take part, sign out of Game Center in Settings. The game works the same without it.

What we don't do

No accounts of our own, no analytics, ads, tracking, or third-party SDKs. Apart from Game Center, the iOS app makes no network connections.

Game controllers

When you connect a controller, the game reads its buttons to let you play and sets its light bar and rumble. None of this is recorded or sent anywhere.

Children

Hexgen collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
